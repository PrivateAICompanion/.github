## What these products do

You pick a ready character or build one. You send messages. You can stay in the same thread so earlier details are more likely to return. Images of the character and voice replies are usually behind paid plans. The free start is short on most sites.

The difference between platforms is mostly memory, how fast the free tier ends, and how clear the paid pricing is. Most run in the browser. Some have apps. Almost all ask you to confirm age.

---

## How people usually start

1. Open the site and pick one ready character.  
2. Send five to ten messages and see if the tone fits.  
3. If it fits, stay in that thread or open the builder.  
4. If it does not fit, close the tab. The free tier is the test.

Paid plans usually add more messages, pictures and voice. Memory is better on paid. Free tiers forget sooner. Media often uses a meter that runs out.

## What to check before you pay

| Check | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Free message limit | A handful is not a daily allowance |
| What paid unlocks | More chat, images, voice, or just higher limits |
| Renewal price | First month is often discounted |
| Cancel path | Account page or support ticket |
| Personal data | Do not put real names or numbers in the chat |

A few days of free use is usually enough. After that the choice is about media and how long you want to stay in one thread.

---

## Common styles

- Ready characters you open in one click.  
- A builder for name, look and short background.  
- One thread you can return to.  
- Pictures and voice on higher plans.  
- Browser first, app later.

None of these is a general assistant. The product is built around a character.
